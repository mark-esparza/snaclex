# Transcription (basic) — an example scene for the SnaCleX Animation Studio.
#
# RNA polymerase II binds a gene's promoter, opens a short "bubble" in the
# double helix, and moves along the DNA building an RNA copy, which peels away
# behind it. Polymerase shape: yeast RNA polymerase II, PDB 1I6H (its two
# largest subunits, chains A and B). Everything else is stylised.

title "Transcription (basic)"
style dark
note "Stylised; not to scale"
credits on

# --- actors ---
actor dna = helix turns=30 color=pink label="DNA"
actor promoter = label text="promoter" on=dna@0.2
actor pol = protein from=1I6H chain=A,B color=gold scale=0.45 label="RNA polymerase II"
actor rna = strand length=40 color=blue rot=0,0,135 hidden=yes label="mRNA"

# --- the gene and its promoter ---
0s      caption "DNA: two strands wound into a double helix" for=4s
2-5s    pol enter from=top to=dna@0.2
4.5s    caption "RNA polymerase II binds the promoter, the start of a gene" for=4s
5-7s    camera zoom pol distance=70

# --- opening the bubble ---
7-9s    dna unwind from=0.17 to=0.23
9s      caption "It opens a short stretch of the helix: the transcription bubble" for=4s

# --- elongation: the RNA grows as the polymerase moves ---
9-9.5s  rna move to=pol
9.5s    rna attach to=pol
10-11s  rna show
10-30s  rna grow to=1 ease=linear
12-30s  pol move to=dna@0.75 ease=linear
12-30s  dna unwind from=0.72 to=0.78 ease=linear
12-30s  camera follow pol
13.5s   caption "Reading one DNA strand as a template, it builds a complementary RNA strand" for=5s
19.5s   caption "The bubble travels with the polymerase; the helix closes again behind it" for=5s
25.5s   caption "The new RNA peels away from the DNA as it grows" for=4s

# --- termination ---
30s     rna detach
30-32s  pol leave to=top
30-33s  dna rewind
30.5s   caption "At the end of the gene, the polymerase lets go and the DNA rewinds" for=4s
33-36s  rna leave to=top
34-37s  camera wide
35s     caption "The messenger RNA (mRNA) goes on to be processed and translated into protein" for=4s
